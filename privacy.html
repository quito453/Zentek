Privacy policy

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Zentek Solutions ("Zentek", "we", "us"), based in Guatemala City, Guatemala. For any privacy question, contact us at the email address listed on our contact page.

2. Information we collect

3. How we use it

We do not sell your personal information and we do not add you to marketing lists without your consent.

4. Service providers

We use trusted providers to run this site: GitHub Pages (hosting), Formspree (contact-form delivery) and, if enabled, Google Analytics. These providers process data on our behalf and may store it outside Guatemala, including in the United States.

5. How long we keep it

We keep contact requests for as long as needed to respond and manage the business relationship, and for no longer than 24 months after our last interaction unless we have an active contract with you.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by writing to the email above. We will respond within 30 days.
