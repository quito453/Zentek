Frequently asked questions

Do we need to buy a monitoring tool?

No. We can use your current tool or deploy an open-source stack such as Zabbix or Grafana for you.

What happens at 3 a.m. when something breaks?

An engineer triages the alert, runs the agreed runbook and, if needed, calls your on-call contact following the escalation matrix.

Can you monitor devices on our private network?

Yes, through a lightweight collector installed inside your network that sends data out over an encrypted connection.
