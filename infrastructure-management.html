Frequently asked questions

Do you replace our internal IT person?

Not necessarily. Many clients keep an internal IT lead and use us for routine administration, after-hours work and specialized tasks.

Will we own the documentation?

Yes. Runbooks, diagrams and asset inventories are stored in your systems and remain yours.

Can you manage on-premise servers?

Yes, remotely, as long as secure remote access is available. When hands-on work is needed we coordinate with a local partner.
