How we work

A clear path from first call to steady operation.

Outsourcing fails when the handoff is rushed. Our process starts small, proves quality with real data and only then scales.

01

Discovery

Week 1

We review your ticket volumes, tools, hours and pain points, and look at sample tickets or systems.

Deliverable

Proposal with scope, SLAs and pricing

02

Pilot

Weeks 2–6

A small team starts on a defined slice of the work, with success criteria agreed up front.

Deliverable

Pilot scorecard against the agreed criteria

03

Transition

Weeks 6–10

Knowledge transfer, runbooks and access setup; then we scale the team to full scope.

Deliverable

Runbooks, escalation matrix and access register

04

Operate & improve

Ongoing

Daily operation against SLAs, with regular reviews and continuous process improvement.

Deliverable

Monthly KPI report and quarterly business review

Timelines are typical for a team of up to 10 people and depend on training needs and access approvals.

Communication cadence

You always know what is happening, without chasing anyone.

Daily Shift handoff notes and live escalations in your Slack or Teams.
Weekly 30-minute check-in with your account lead: open issues, priorities, feedback.
Monthly KPI report against your SLA, with trends and improvement actions.
Quarterly Business review: results, roadmap, staffing and budget for the next quarter.

How we protect quality

Quality is managed as a process, not left to individual effort.

Hiring & training

English assessment, technical tests and product certification before anyone goes live on your account.

QA & coaching

Weekly scoring of interactions and calibration sessions with your team.

Continuous improvement

Root-cause analysis of recurring issues and knowledge-base updates every month.

Security & confidentiality

We follow the controls your security team expects from a vendor with access to your systems, and we are happy to complete your vendor security questionnaire.

  • NDA with the company and every team member
  • Individual accounts with MFA and least-privilege access to your systems
  • Managed workstations with disk encryption and endpoint protection
  • Documented offboarding: access revoked the same day
  • Your data stays in your systems — we work inside your tools
