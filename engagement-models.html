Engagement models

Three ways to work with Zentek.

Choose the model that matches how predictable your workload is and how much day-to-day control you want. You can combine them — for example, a dedicated support team plus managed NOC monitoring.

Dedicated team

Monthly rate per FTE

Full-time agents, engineers or developers who work only on your account. You set the priorities; we handle recruiting, payroll, equipment, training and people management.

Best for

  • Ongoing bilingual customer support
  • Extending a software team
  • Work that needs deep product knowledge

Managed service

Per user, device or ticket volume

We own the outcome against agreed SLAs — response times, resolution times and uptime. You don't manage people; you review results in a monthly report.

Best for

  • IT helpdesk for your employees
  • Infrastructure and cloud administration
  • 24/7 network monitoring (NOC)

Project-based

Fixed price per milestone

A defined deliverable with a fixed price and timeline. We agree on scope and acceptance criteria up front and invoice as milestones are delivered.

Best for

  • Migrations to Microsoft 365 or the cloud
  • APIs, integrations and new applications
  • Setting up a helpdesk or monitoring platform

What affects pricing

Every proposal is built on these factors, so you only pay for the coverage and skills you need.

  • Languages required
  • Coverage hours (8x5 vs 24/7)
  • Seniority and technical skills
  • Volume of tickets, users or devices
  • Tools, training and product complexity

Frequently asked questions

Can we combine models?

Yes. A common setup is a dedicated support team plus a managed helpdesk or NOC service, under one contract and one monthly invoice.

Can we switch models later?

Yes. Many clients start with a project or pilot and move to a dedicated team or managed service once the work is predictable.

In what currency do you invoice?

In US dollars, monthly, by bank transfer. Project work is invoiced by milestone.
