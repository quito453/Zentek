Why Guatemala works for US companies

Central Standard Time, all year

Guatemala is on UTC−6 and does not observe daylight saving time: the same time as US Central in winter and one hour behind it in summer.

A short flight away

Direct flights connect Guatemala City with several US hubs, including Miami, Houston, Atlanta and Los Angeles.

Bilingual workforce

A growing pool of English-speaking professionals with experience in contact centers, IT support and software.

Trade ties with the US

Guatemala is part of CAFTA-DR, the free trade agreement between the US, Central America and the Dominican Republic.

Cultural proximity

Familiarity with US business culture and native Spanish for customers across the Americas.

Simple to contract

Contracts in English and monthly invoices in US dollars.
