Frequently asked questions

Will agents use our brand name?

Yes. Agents represent your company, using your tone of voice, email signatures and help-desk accounts. Your customers talk to your team, not to a vendor.

How long does training take?

Typically one to two weeks, depending on product complexity. We build the training material with your team and certify agents before they go live.

Can we start with only chat or email?

Yes. Many clients start with written channels and add voice once the team is up to speed.
