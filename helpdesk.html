Frequently asked questions

Can you support our remote employees in the US?

Yes. Helpdesk work is remote by design. For on-site hardware tasks we coordinate with your local vendor or your ship-and-swap process.

Do you need admin access to our systems?

Only the access needed for the agreed scope, granted through individual accounts with MFA, so every action is traceable.

Can you work white-label for an MSP?

Yes. We can answer under your MSP's name and work inside your PSA or ticketing tool.
