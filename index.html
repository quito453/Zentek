    Frequently asked questions

    What is the minimum engagement?

    You can start with a single dedicated person or a 30-day pilot. We scale the team as volume and confidence grow.

    How fast can you start?

    Most pilots begin within 2–4 weeks of signing, depending on team size and the training your product requires.

    Do you use our tools or yours?

    Yours, whenever possible — your ticketing system, chat, monitoring and code repositories. If you don't have them yet, we can recommend and set them up.

    Can you cover nights and weekends?

    Yes. Helpdesk and NOC services are available 8x5, extended hours or 24/7, priced according to the coverage window.

    How do you invoice?

    Monthly, in US dollars, by bank transfer. Project work is invoiced by milestone.
